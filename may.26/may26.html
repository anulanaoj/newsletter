time moves slow, it is stretched and i feel stretching fingers alongate and u-turn towards themselves. RETRACTION moving forward

i notice only after looking at the work i illustrate movement, a friend told me i was gripping a body tightly - he always has good associations -, i think i crawl, i do it attentively. when i trace my hand i must keep it tense so the pen doesn’t change it’s position, and keep the legs tense so they won’t move by themselves. i feel discomfort, i keep the body warm at night. i dont feel the right side of the tip of the right hand indicating finger.

if the pen isn’t able to touch my body i close one eye and keep its top part on the edge of the arm or leg, this is some indicator (not to get a taller pen, i think it is to give closer of a look). i hold the body with attention. the works that force me to come closer to them and be precise give me opportunity to practice attention. i’ve allowed this to happen due to, for a dense time, being downstairs cutting out the shape of my body from two meter long osb plates, (i was angry when i remembered i needed to sand them as well). and the sanding was, (apologies for my lack of sense), arduous. i wasn’t to understand before how it does something to the brain when you hold yourself at the hip, or balance yourself together to be free-standing.
